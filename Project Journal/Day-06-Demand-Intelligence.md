# Day 6 — Demand Intelligence Dashboard

## Objective
Build a demand-focused Power BI dashboard to analyze ride demand patterns, peak hours, daily trends, monthly demand, and geographic demand distribution.

## Dashboard Page
**Page Name:** Demand Intelligence

## KPI
- Peak Hour Demand

## Dashboard Visuals

--> 1. Trips by Pickup Hour
- Visual: Line Chart
- X-axis: Pickup Hour
- Measure: Total Trips
- Purpose: Analyze demand variation throughout the day.

--> 2. Trips by Day of Week
- Visual: Clustered Column Chart
- X-axis: Day Name
- Measure: Total Trips
- Purpose: Identify daily demand patterns.

--> 3. Trips by Pickup Location
- Visual: Clustered Bar Chart
- Y-axis: Pickup Location
- Measure: Total Trips
- Purpose: Identify high-demand pickup locations.

--> 4. Monthly Demand Trend
- Visual: Line Chart
- X-axis: Month Name
- Measure: Total Trips
- Purpose: Analyze monthly demand patterns.

--> 5. Trips by Day Type
- Visual: Clustered Column Chart
- X-axis: Day Type
- Measure: Total Trips
- Purpose: Compare weekday and weekend demand.

--> 6. Trips by Zone
- Visual: Clustered Bar Chart
- Y-axis: Zone
- Measure: Total Trips
- Purpose: Identify high-demand geographic zones.

## Interactive Slicers
- Day Name
- Pickup Hour
- Zone

## Dashboard Formatting
- Applied professional blue and navy color theme.
- Used light blue for secondary categories.
- Applied consistent visual alignment and spacing.
- Added light dashboard background.
- Formatted charts and slicers for readability.
- Maintained consistent visual styling.

## Validation
- Tested all slicers and visual interactions.
- Verified hourly demand from 0–23.
- Verified chronological ordering of monthly demand.
- Checked day-of-week ordering.
- Checked location and zone sorting by Total Trips.
- Confirmed no visual errors or overlapping elements.

## Conclusion
The Demand Intelligence dashboard provides an interactive view of ride demand patterns across time and geography. It helps identify peak demand periods, high-demand locations, and differences between weekday and weekend activity.
